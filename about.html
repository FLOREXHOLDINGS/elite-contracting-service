About

A family business, since 2011.

Elite Contracting Service is a property service company — homes and commercial properties across the Finger Lakes region of New York.

The standard

Craftsmanship is the level of the work.

The company was built on a simple read of the trade: the work is the advertisement. Show up dressed for it, photograph everything, show the customer the photos, and give one number for the whole correction. The number doesn't move at the door.

That standard was set by the generation that built this company. It's carried by the next one — the same structure, every job, varied only for situation and circumstance.
