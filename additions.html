The look

What the inspection or estimate reads.

  • The site — slope, access, and where the addition ties into the existing house
  • The structure: foundation type, framing, and how the roof line meets the old one
  • The finish level inside, matched to the rooms it joins

The photos on this page are one company's run: an ICF foundation going up on stepped footings, and a finished addition — siding, windows, door — that reads as part of the house it joined. The interior finish is held to the same standard as the carpentry work: crown, hardwood, and doors that match the existing rooms.

One story only. That's a limit we state before the estimate, not a discovery made during it — if your project needs a second story, we'll tell you plainly at the first conversation.
