Cleaning

A cleaning division with five years of its own work behind it — and a rate stated as plainly as every other price in this company.

How it starts: a free estimate, and the rate is the rate. $74.75 per cleaner per hour. Crew size is settled before the job, and the per-cleaner rate is stated on the quote — no arithmetic hidden in a flat number.

Regular and recurring cleaning, deep cleans, move-in and move-out, lake-home and rental turnover, one-time and spring cleans. Typical regular visits: 1–2 bed $299, 3 bed $448.50, 4+ bed $598. The rate doesn't change with frequency — no discount games.
