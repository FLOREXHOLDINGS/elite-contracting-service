The look

What the inspection or estimate reads.

  • Every entry point, not just the one the animal was seen using
  • Failed seals and soft repairs — foam stuffed in a gap is a before photo, not a repair
  • Deck and porch skirting, foundation lines, rooflines, chimneys, vents
  • The material each opening needs — mesh, metal, masonry, or carpentry

The sequence at the top of this page is one real job: an open framed void under a deck with a burrow dug in front of it, the mesh going over the openings, and the finished run — clean, tight, and painted into the structure. That's the standard every exclusion job is photographed against: before, during, after.

Foam is the failure we replace most. It's soft, it shrinks, and animals chew through it in a night. A real exclusion is fitted material, fastened to structure — the gloved-hand and mesh photos here are what that looks like up close.

Straight answers

Asked, and answered plainly.

Are exclusion estimates free?

Yes — repair pricing is a free estimate, in person, after the look. One number for the whole correction.

Do you guarantee the animal stays out?

Coverage for the property is reviewed with you before service begins, so what's covered is in writing before the work starts — not promised loosely on a page.

Can exclusion be booked without a removal?

Yes. If the animals are already out — or were never in — the entries can be closed on their own.
