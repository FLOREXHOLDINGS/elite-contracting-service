Insulation

Attic insulation removal and installation — both, or either one alone. Crawlspaces and basements too.

How it starts: a $189 inspection. Insulation is priced after a $189 inspection — in person, after the look. The fee is $189 on every residential insulation call and $245.70 on commercial properties, and it is not negotiable. One number for the whole job follows the inspection.

Insulation is the owner's own trade — fifteen years in it, carried alongside the wildlife and pest work. It is priced the way a trade is priced: after a proper inspection, never from a photograph or a phone call.

Our standard in attics is blown-in cellulose. Fiberglass where it's requested, and in crawlspaces and basements.

Removal runs as a contained system: the old insulation comes out contained, the space is disinfected with a hospital-grade disinfectant, and the new insulation goes in. Photographs at every stage — you see what came out and what went in.

Old insulation that's been lived in by animals is the common case. If wildlife is part of the picture, the removal and the exclusion are planned together, in one number.

The look

What the inspection or estimate reads.

  • Contaminated or animal-destroyed insulation — droppings, nesting, latrines, seed caches
  • Entry gaps at rim joists, eaves, dormers, and foundation lines
  • Fallen or missing insulation in crawlspaces and basement bays
  • Moisture and the damage that travels with it

The removal runs contained: the old insulation comes out bagged, not scattered through your house, and the space is disinfected with a hospital-grade disinfectant before anything new goes in. Every stage is photographed — what came out, what the space looked like empty, and what went back in.

If animals caused the damage, the insulation work and the exclusion are planned together. New insulation over an open entry point is just clean bedding for the next tenant; the entry gets closed in the same plan, for one number.
