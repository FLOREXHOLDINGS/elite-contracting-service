Straight answers

Asked, and answered plainly.

Are inspections free?

Many are, for homeowners: rodent inspections are free, squirrels and chipmunks inside the house are free, and the check for more bats is free. Where a fee applies it is $189 — wildlife confirmed inside or under a structure, squirrels and chipmunks seen only outside, insulation on every call, and any inspection for college housing, a rental property, or a commercial property. A paid fee never comes off the invoice — the inspection is its own service. Repair and addition pricing is a free estimate.

Will you price a job over the phone?

Set-price work is on the rate card. Everything else is priced in person, after the look and the photos — one number for the whole correction.

Do you use poison for rodents?

No. No poison, ever — traps, photographed, and the entry closed.

Where do you work?

The Finger Lakes Region of New York, the Greater Rochester Area, the Syracuse area, and the Southern Tier. Buffalo is not in it. Norwich is not in it.
