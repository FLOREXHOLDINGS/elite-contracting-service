The look

What the inspection or estimate reads.

  • The vent itself — type, condition, and what's nesting in it
  • Height and access, which set the tier before the truck rolls
  • Roosting patterns on roofs, ledges, and under solar arrays
  • Waste build-up, and what sanitization the site needs

The three photos aren't paperwork — they're how the tier gets set honestly. A vent that reads as one story from the ground can be two from the ladder; the wide shot shows the height, the close-up shows the vent, and the ground shot shows what the ladder stands on. The price you pay is the tier the photos prove.

For solar arrays, birds nesting underneath is a wiring and fire problem, not just a mess. The proofing is installed with AAC Distributing bird products, fitted around the array so the birds stay out from under the panels. Roosting exclusion on commercial buildings is quoted from the $245.70 commercial inspection.

Straight answers

Asked, and answered plainly.

Why do you need photos before scheduling?

Because the tier is set by height and access, and the photos prove both. No vent job is scheduled until they're in.

Is nest removal included in the tier price?

No — nest removal and disinfection is +$89, added to the vent removal and stated before scheduling.

Can you keep birds off a roof or out from under solar panels?

Yes — roosting exclusion and solar-panel proofing are part of this service, quoted after the bird inspection — $189 residential, $245.70 commercial.
