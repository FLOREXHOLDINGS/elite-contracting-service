Pricing

The Rate Card.

Set prices. They don't move at the door, and they don't move on the phone. Every figure is shown with its terms — never bare.

Wildlife inspection
$149

Charged when the animal is confirmed inside the structure or burrowing under a structure — a deck, a porch, a shed. When it isn't confirmed, the call goes straight to a removal period and there is no inspection fee. Chipmunks are the exception in reverse: confirmed inside the house, the inspection is free; an exterior chipmunk problem carries the $149.

Why the card reads this way

The number is the last thing you hear.

Every job in this company runs the same order: we come out and look, we photograph everything, we show you the photos, and then the number. Set prices are printed above because those jobs are the same job every time. Everything else is priced from the photos of your house — not guessed from a phone call, and not moved at the door.
