Pricing

The Rate Card.

Set prices. They don't move at the door, and they don't move on the phone. Every figure is shown with its terms — never bare.

Inspections
$189

One fee wherever an inspection is charged: $189. Many inspections are free to the homeowner — rodent inspections (mice and rats) are free; a squirrel or chipmunk inside the house or in its walls is free; and bat inspections are free — bats in the attic, or a bat seen anywhere in the house. The $189 fee applies to college housing and to rental properties — commercial properties are charged $245.70; to squirrels and chipmunks seen only outside the house; and to raccoon, skunk, groundhog, and opossum confirmed inside the structure or burrowing under one — a deck, a porch, a shed. When wildlife is not confirmed, the call goes straight to a removal period and there is no inspection fee.

Repair pricing is a free estimate — the number is given in person, after the look, one number for the whole job.

Inspections and estimates, once, plainly

An inspection is a separate service: a truck, a trip, and a man who knows what he's looking at. A paid inspection fee never comes off an invoice, for anything.

Where an inspection is free, it is a courtesy extended to homeowners — rodent inspections, bat inspections, squirrels and chipmunks inside the house. College housing and rental properties are charged the standard fee of $189; commercial properties are charged $245.70.

Repairs are different. Repair pricing is a free estimate.

Commercial properties

Commercial work is charged above the residential card, by a set percentage for each service: inspections, wildlife removal, dead animal removal, and snake inspections at 30% above; rodent work at 40% above, applied when the job is priced at the inspection; bat removal at 50% above; bird vent work at 25% above; and insulation at 25% above on small commercial jobs, with large projects quoted by scope. The commercial figure for every set price is printed beside it on this card. Bat, dead animal, and large bird or insulation work on commercial buildings is quoted after inspection at those percentages as the minimum, because the building sets the work. Commercial rodent inspections may be waived case by case. Commercial bat inspections are a minimum of $489, and rise with the size and nature of the structure.

Cleaning is the exception: the $74.75 rate is the rate for homes and businesses alike.

Why the card reads this way

The number is the last thing you hear.

Every job in this company runs the same order: we come out and look, we photograph everything, we show you the photos, and then the number. Set prices are printed above because those jobs are the same job every time. Everything else is priced from the photos of your house — not guessed from a phone call, and not moved at the door.
